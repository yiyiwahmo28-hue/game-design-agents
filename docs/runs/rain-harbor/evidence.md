# Rain Harbor — executed baseline and revision evidence

Generated by `node scripts/harbor-evidence.mjs --write` from both public fictional configurations and the shared engine.
The follow-up request enables one undo; original configuration bytes and economic parameters are checked against the first-run record.

## Executed rules

- 7 forward turns; initial fuel 4. Refuel gains 2, or 5 after preparation. Preparation costs 2 fuel and one turn.
- Every accepted forward action spends one turn. The final action resolves before closure.
- Revised manual play can undo the last successful forward action once, including after an ending; undo restores its prior game state and spends no turn or fuel.
- The spent allowance is outside the restored snapshot. Failed actions do not replace history.

## Actual revision checks

Statuses come from assertions executed during report generation. Concrete states and events are retained under `executions` in the JSON.

- **passed: Original configuration preserved.** The baseline bytes match the SHA-256 recorded before the change request.
- **passed: Economic parameters unchanged.** Turn budget, fuel, preparation and every boat cost/passenger count match the original.
- **passed: Preparation restored completely.** Prepare then undo restores turn, fuel, preparation and both fuel ledgers; the allowance stays spent.
- **passed: Refuel restored completely.** Undo restores ordinary and prepared refuel without keeping gained fuel.
- **passed: Rescue restored completely.** Prepare, refuel, rescue the trawler, then undo returns to turn 2, fuel 7 and zero rescued people.
- **passed: Endings can be reopened.** A storm ending and a one-boat all-rescued fixture both reopen; the undo allowance is spent.
- **passed: Allowance cannot be restored.** A second undo fails immediately and after another successful action; a new run alone receives a new allowance.
- **passed: Failures preserve the last success.** Repeated preparation, unaffordable rescue, unknown input and closed-harbor attempts preserve history and resources.
- **passed: Undo respects eligibility.** The original has no undo; a new revised run cannot undo before its first success.
- **passed: Forward policy trajectories preserved.** All three policies reproduce the originally observed actions and full economic trajectories; none chooses undo.

## Preparation and rescue tradeoff

The forward-action optimizer visited 217 physical states. Maximum rescued passengers: 18; with preparation prohibited: 16.
The revised optimizer also excludes undo and its histories. It does not enumerate every revised state.
Undo returns to an earlier reachable position without retaining the discarded action benefit. It cannot improve the ideal-information passenger maximum in this fixed deterministic model; human mistake recovery remains unmeasured.

Legal first-action comparisons (later forward choices optimized for the passenger objective):

- refuel: 0 people immediately; best final count 16.
- prepare: 0 people immediately; best final count 18.
- rescue:dinghy: 2 people immediately; best final count 18.
- rescue:skiff: 4 people immediately; best final count 16.

Reachable late-choice counterexample:

After rescue:skiff → refuel → rescue:dinghy → refuel → refuel → refuel, turn 6, fuel 6, 6 people rescued:
- Prepare now: final 6 people.
- rescue:trawler now: final 12 people.
Both are actual transitions from the same reachable state. Preparing is not optimal in every state.

## Reproduce

`node --test tests/harbor.test.mjs`

`node scripts/harbor-evidence.mjs --write`

`node scripts/harbor-evidence.mjs --check`

`--check` reruns actual scenarios and compares both reports byte for byte. Missing/stale files or failed scenarios cause a nonzero exit. Reports contain no execution timestamp or machine-specific path.

## Limits

- The core delegate combined systems, economy and implementation responsibilities; its local tests and this report are self-checks.
- The optimizer searches forward actions only, excluding undo histories; its optimum concerns the stated passenger objective.
- This script records deterministic model execution, not browser observation or human playtests.
- No improvement in fun, human comprehension or multi-agent effectiveness has been measured.
