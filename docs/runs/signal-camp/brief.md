# Signal Camp — the actual redesign brief

## Source and authorization

The maintainer requested this case on 2026-10-13:

> 可以，就按“总资源产出不变，让前期等待变成有意义的选择”做一个完整案例，重点体现我的策划经验和修改前后的差异，再更新首页 Demo 和 GitHub。

Translation: create a complete case in which total resource production stays
unchanged while early waiting becomes meaningful choice; emphasize the design
reasoning and before/after difference, then update the homepage demo and GitHub.

This is an original fictional exercise. The initial waiting-heavy design is
constructed to isolate the requested problem. It is not a recovered commercial
game, a customer case, a measured player complaint, or an external user study.
The revision was requested in this brief, not invented as a later user message.

## Required outcome

- Keep a playable original and a playable revision.
- Define the resource, comparison horizon and accounting before making claims.
- Preserve total production; use the stronger condition of identical income
  after every corresponding game turn, irrespective of legal action choices.
- Move a consequential allocation decision earlier. Show different outcomes,
  an opportunity cost, and a context in which the preferred route reverses.
- Show the design alternatives considered and the costs accepted.
- Execute the code and browser controls, retain review findings and repairs,
  and make the evidence reproducible.
- Explain the method in reusable terms, without presenting this example's
  numbers as universal game-design standards.

## Scoped design decisions

These are coordinator-approved choices for this public case, not requirements
supplied by the user and not inherited from a private project:

- Six discrete game turns; the page lets the visitor advance them through
  actual actions. A turn is not a measured minute of human waiting.
- Three initial supplies. Every accepted action consumes one turn and is
  followed by one supply delivery. Six supplies are produced over a full run;
  initial stock plus production gives nine available supplies.
- Supplies are the only spendable resource. People helped and routes mapped
  are separate mission outcomes; they never create spendable supplies.
- A six-supply bundle unlocks two tools in the original. The revision also
  permits buying either three-supply tool first. Both mission costs remain one.
- Rescue and mapping have an explicitly announced early window. The task
  objective is known before the initial allocation; no hidden goal is used to
  punish the player after choosing.
- Complete runs continue through all six turns. Failed actions must not change
  the turn, income, spending, balance or mission state.

## Acceptance and limits

Compare original and revised rules from the same initial state and horizon.
Enumerate legal outcomes, report both objectives, and distinguish an available
button from a non-dominated choice. Do not claim that waiting disappears, all
actions are equally useful, the player enjoys the result, or the workflow beats
a single assistant without the appropriate evidence.

The model can support a claim about when choices and consequences become
available. Human understanding and preference remain questions for a playtest.
